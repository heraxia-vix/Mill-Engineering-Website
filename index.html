Engineering Process

A consistent process, applied to every engagement.

Seven steps, followed in order, regardless of industry or scale.

1

Understand

Listen to the operating context before proposing anything.

2

Study

Study operating conditions, constraints and performance objectives.

3

Evaluate

Evaluate technologies and alternatives objectively.

4

Identify

Identify the engineering approach that fits the application.

5

Engineer

Engineer the solution with attention to reliability and lifecycle value.

6

Execute

Support execution with technical coordination.

7

Support

Remain available throughout the asset lifecycle.
